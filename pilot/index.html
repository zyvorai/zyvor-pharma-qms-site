Pilot

Prove the fit in an afternoon.

A short plan for manufacturers evaluating Zyvor Pharma QMS with synthetic data. Pilot, not validated, not 21 CFR Part 11 / Annex 11.

Start with the live demo
  1. Look first. Open the live demo: shared, read-only, synthetic data. It shows the screens but cannot be changed, so to create records you need your own instance.
  2. Stand up your own instance. Ask us for an evaluation bundle (none is published yet), or use compose, k3s, FluxVM or a desktop launcher. Keep a default instance on your own machine; for anything shared, enable strict authentication.
  3. Scope one product. Pick a solid oral or simple dosage form and invent a batch number.
  4. Walk the spine. Draft, in progress, laboratory, quality review, approve. Run the guided eBMR in order with second-person verification of critical steps, tick the release checklist, and print the executed batch record PDF.
  5. Record a lab result. Pass one assay, fail one dissolution, open OOS, second-person verify the pass.
  6. Close the loop. Raise a deviation and CAPA and complete a CAPA effectiveness check. Try an approval workflow (pilot) with a staged approver.
  7. Touch your market. Draft an APQR (India), FAR (US) or QP certification (EU) for the same batch. For India, work through the Revised Schedule M readiness checklist as a self-assessment and try the inspection kit export.
  8. Check the IT side. Strict mode sign-in, single sign-on if you use it, and the honest limits on the security page: partial site scoping, uneven role checks, single node.
  9. Decide what a real pilot must prove. Validation ownership stays with you. The software is not validated, and the starter validation templates are not executed evidence.
