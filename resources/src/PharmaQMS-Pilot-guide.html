What a pilot should prove

1. Healthcurl -sf http://127.0.0.1:8080/healthz returns ok.
2. Catalogue/api/v1/features lists 50+ modules and markets india / us / eu / global.
3. Batch pathAs mfg, create a batch; as lab, enter results; as qa, approve / release.
4. OOSTrigger an out-of-spec result and walk the OOS record.
5. MarketsOpen Markets — draft an APQR (India) or APR (US) / PQR (EU) stub.
6. StopQuit the app or run installer down. Data stays under ~/.zyvor-pharma-qms / your Postgres.

Decision questions

Does the spine match your plant?

Batch → lab → QA without exporting to Excel mid-flight.

Can IT host it?

Compose, FluxVM, k3s or Windows/WSL — pick one path.

What must validation cover?

You own IQ/OQ/PQ. This product is not validated for you.

What integrations matter?

ERP / LIMS connectors are not built; plan them as a phase-2 ask.
