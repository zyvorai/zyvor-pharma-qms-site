Executive overview

Batch to release for manufacturers.

Zyvor Pharma QMS is Zyvor AI Labs’ manufacturing quality system: one spine from batch and eBMR through laboratory and QA to release and recall — with India Schedule M, US FDA and EU GMP market modules. Distinct from retail Zyvora Pharmacy.

84catalogue entries: 73 modules + 11 AI assistants
18market / regulatory modules + Schedule M checklist
5deploy paths (desktop, compose, FluxVM, k3s, Windows)

What it covers

ManufacturingBatches, recipes, guided eBMR (forced step order, second-person verification of critical steps), executed batch record PDF, IPC, line clearance, cleaning, packaging, retains.
LaboratorySamples, results, specs, automatic OOS, second-person verify.
QualityDeviations, CAPA with effectiveness checks, complaints, audits, risk, QA approvals, release checklist, supplier qualification and scorecards.
TraceabilityGenealogy, CoA draft, quarantine, recalls.
MarketsAPQR / APR / PQR, self-inspection, PV, field alerts, EU QP, serialization, GDP, licences, water, ALCOA+; Revised Schedule M readiness checklist (self-assessment), inspection kit export, GS1 / QR payloads.
Plant opsStability, environment, warehouse, documents, training, change control, equipment.
PlatformApproval workflows, saved reports, ERP / LIMS connector framework, offline queue, passkeys, SAML / OIDC SSO, SCIM; several are pilot-flagged and site scoping is partial.

Why organisations evaluate it

One release spine

Manufacturing, lab and QA share the same batch record instead of three spreadsheets.

Market-aware pilot

India, US and EU modules as electronic records — not live gateway filings.

Yours to run

Self-hosted Postgres + Go API. Desktop launchers, compose, FluxVM or k3s; single node, not production-hardened.

Honest by design

Pilot-ready status and non-claims are printed on the product, the site and this sheet.

Live demo. A shared, read-only demo on synthetic data is at pharma.zyvor.dev (details on the Try it page). It may be reset or changed and is not for real data.

Status. Pilot electronic records only. Not a validated system. Not 21 CFR Part 11 / Annex 11 / WHO certified. Synthetic or non-regulated demonstration data. Do not use for regulated release without your own validation.
