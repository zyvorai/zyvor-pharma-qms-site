India readiness aids

  • Revised Schedule M readiness checklist: 17 clauses, each pointing to the module that holds evidence, with status, owner, evidence note and due date. Self-assessment only: no compliance determination, not a CDSCO filing; the clause list is a paraphrase to check against the current notified text. Shared across sites.
  • Inspection kit export: zip of JSON records (deviations, training, self-inspections, regulatory inspections, product quality reviews, Schedule M) plus a manifest, for inspection preparation. No PDF, no attachments, no date or product filter, no role check beyond sign-in. Not a regulatory submission.
  • GS1 / QR label payloads: builds a GS1 element string (GTIN, batch, dates) with sanity checks. No barcode or QR image is rendered, the GTIN check digit is not validated, and there is no printer or GS1 registry integration. Not a CDSCO-validated encoding.

Deliberately out of scope here

  • GST / e-invoice / GSTR — commercial tax; belongs in ERP or retail billing, not manufacturing QMS.
  • Live CDSCO SUGAM, FDA ESG, EMVS, NMVS, or DSCSA hub connectivity.
  • Full PSUR authoring or validated submission packs.

Pilot stubs only. Mapping language helps evaluators navigate; it is not a claim that the software is Schedule M / FDA / EU GMP compliant.
